Our mission

Automation that feels
like a superpower

Freelancers and creatives are among the most skilled people in the world. But the tools they inherit — manual invoicing, scattered client comms, copy-paste workflows — weren't built for how they work. Rumuni is.

We obsess over understanding your process before we write a single line of code. Every automation, every integration, every website we build is designed around how you actually work — not how a software manual thinks you should.

Our north star is simple: when a Rumuni client finishes onboarding, they get their time back. What they do with it is entirely up to them.

Our journey

From side project
to creative infrastructure

2022

The first workflow

Alex built an automation pipeline for his own freelance studio — cutting invoice time from 4 hours a week to 10 minutes. Friends asked for the same. Rumuni was born.

2023

Team and product

Sam and Maya joined. We formalised our automation service, launched our first web development offering, and onboarded our first 50 clients across design, photography, and content creation.

2024

AI capabilities

We integrated AI-powered tools into our service stack — enabling clients to auto-draft proposals, sort inquiries, and generate content briefs at scale.

2025

200+ creatives served

Over 200 freelancers and creative studios now run on Rumuni-built workflows. We're expanding into agency partnerships and multi-user team plans.
