Our Heritage

Born from the
Heart of Bantayan

Islas Bantayan was founded in 2013 by a team of passionate islanders who believed that Bantayan's beauty and culinary richness deserved to be shared with the world in the most luxurious way possible.

What began as a small beachside catering service has grown into the premier luxury catering and tourism company in the Visayas region — trusted by hundreds of couples, travelers, and corporate clients from around the globe.

Our roots run deep into Filipino tradition, while our vision looks forward — blending heritage flavors with contemporary culinary artistry, and island adventures with five-star comfort.

Passion

Every dish and every tour is made with genuine love for our island home and the people we serve.

Sustainability

We work with local communities and maintain eco-responsible practices in all our operations.

Excellence

We hold ourselves to the highest standards — no compromise on quality, service, or experience.

Integrity

Transparent pricing, honest communication, and a deep commitment to every client's trust.
